Mouse Haunt Mouse Haunt is a project that contained several different products within it.
The project revolves around Mice and Ghosts, with all gamemodes tying into a complete ecosystem.
Lock'n'Load, Mouse Haunt's first gamemode, is an idle farming game, successfully launched in 2022.
Dodgeball, its second gamemode, is a team-based competitive online multiplayer, where players compete in heated matches of dodgeball.
Castle Heist, the last gamemode, has the players invading a castle looking for the cursed Cheese, with a twist: players change teams when they die.
Working as Lead Economy Designer, I've helped make this project a reality, working with a team of over 20 people over the course of one year, for a very passionate community of people around the world.

Find it on: Mouse Haunt Whitepaper
Project Galena Project Galena is a captivating single-player colony simulator set during the 1850s silver rush in California.
As a determined pioneer, you'll embark on a quest for fortune and prosperity in the untamed wilderness. Your objective is to establish and expand your own mining colony by making strategic decisions on resource management, infrastructure development, and settler management.

Project Galena is a work in progress from a small team of passionate developers, and I'm thrilled to be a part of it as the Lead Designer and founder.
Coming to Steam in the near future, stay tuned!
